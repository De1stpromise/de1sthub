Bio

The first time I saw a desktop computer running Windows XP, something in my imagination shifted permanently. I was still in primary school, and I remember the feeling clearly. I could not think about anything else. I wanted a computer of my own, I wanted to explore it, and I wanted to know as much about it as I possibly could. That moment planted something that never left. That early pull carried me through school and into Enugu State University of Science and Technology, where I studied computer science and graduated in 2022. It gave structure to what had until then been pure curiosity, and it taught me to think in systems, in logic, and in process. Today that curiosity continues to expand. Between Enugu and Lagos, I am deepening my knowledge across the wider tech space, and alongside it I have grown another branch of who I am. I have a genuine passion for beauty and visual expression, which shows up in videography, writing, and abstract creativity, work I am only now beginning to let others see. It exists as its own space in my life, separate from the technical work but shaped by the same attention to detail. Looking ahead, my focus is turning toward networking and cloud computing. This is the direction I am building myself into, and my current phase is entirely about that, gathering the knowledge and the skills required to reach the highest point of that path. At the very top of that path is a goal I hold with intention. I want to participate in the laying of undersea network and data cables, the infrastructure that quietly holds the modern world together. It is a long horizon, but it is the one I am building toward.
